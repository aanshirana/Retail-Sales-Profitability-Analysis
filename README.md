# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses retail sales and profitability data to identify patterns across product categories, sub-categories, regions, customer segments and discount levels. The analysis focuses on understanding the factors influencing revenue and profitability and identifying areas that may require further investigation.

## Tools Used
- Google Sheets — data cleaning and preparation
- Google BigQuery SQL — querying and analytical exploration
- Tableau Public — data visualisation and dashboard development

## Analysis Performed
- Overall sales and profitability KPIs
- Regional sales and profitability analysis
- Category performance
- Sub-category profitability
- Customer segment performance
- Yearly sales and profit trends
- Discount impact on profitability
- Product-level profitability

## Key Insights
The analysis identified differences in sales and profitability across regions, categories and customer segments. It also highlighted the relationship between discounting and profitability and identified product-level performance patterns that can support further investigation into margin performance.
